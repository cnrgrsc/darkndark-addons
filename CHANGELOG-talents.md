# Changelog — Darkndark Talents

## v0.2.0 (unreleased)
- Minimap button: click to open the window, right-click for a quick menu (Top Players, Gear & Stats, Lay out bars, Undo bars). Drag it around the minimap. The addon is also listed in Blizzard's addon compartment.
- The Gear & Stats problem list now scrolls instead of overflowing the window.
- New **Top Players** tab: for your current spec and each content type, the top 20 players ranked by rating or M+ score, with realm, hero tree and item level. Use any player's exact talents with one click.
- New build chooser when you enter content. You pick the recommended build, the other hero tree, your saved build, a top player's build, or keep your current talents; nothing changes until you choose.
- New **Share** button: puts a clickable talent build link in chat.
- Build data for every class and spec.

## v0.1.0 (unreleased)
- First version for Midnight (12.x).
- Recommended talent builds for Mythic+, raid, Solo Shuffle (1v1), 2v2, 3v3, Blitz and Rated BG, based on what the top 50 players of each spec actually run (from murlok.io and raider.io).
- One click writes the build into a "DND ..." loadout and switches to it. If the recommended build changes, applying it again updates that loadout.
- Offers to switch builds when you enter a dungeon, raid, arena or battleground.
- Save your own talents as the build for any content type.
- Gear check: compares your secondary stats with top players' and flags item level gaps, missing enchants and empty sockets.
- Bar layout: puts the build's spells on your visible action bars and binds keys (rotation 1-6 R F T, cooldowns Shift+1-6, defensives Ctrl+1-5, interrupt G, utility Z X C V). Works with Blizzard bars, ElvUI, Bartender4 and Dominos. Macros and items are left alone, and an undo keeps your last 5 layouts. It can also run automatically after each build change (you confirm the first time).
- Adds a "Darkndark" button to the talent window. The addon window can be moved and resized.
