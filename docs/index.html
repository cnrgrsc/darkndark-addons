Addon 1 · Darkndark

A rotation helper that plays by Midnight's rules

Midnight's Secret Values ended the old rotation helpers. Darkndark starts from Blizzard's Assisted Combat and adds its own layer on top, using only what addons can still read: is a spell ready, usable, in range or procced. Nothing is ever pressed for you.

Next ability, plus what follows

A big icon for the next ability to press and up to four predicted abilities after it, with procs moved to the front.

Your keybinds on every icon

Read from Blizzard bars, ElvUI, Bartender4, Dominos and your macros, so you see the key to press, not just the spell.

Cooldowns, handled your way

Finds your spec's major cooldowns automatically. With “Hold cooldowns”, they glow on their own bar and you decide when to use them.

Defensives at a glance

A separate bar for your personal defensives, greyed out while they're on cooldown.

Never miss a kick

An interrupt icon appears when your target starts casting and glows when your kick is ready, with an optional sound.

Your screen, your layout

Type /dnd unlock, then drag any panel. Use the mouse wheel or the corner grip to resize it. Masque skins are supported.

What Midnight still lets addons read

  • ✓Assisted Combat suggestion: Blizzard's own pick, for every spec
  • ✓Ready or not: whether a cooldown is active (the exact time left is hidden)
  • ✓Usable, in range, procced: resources, distance, spell glows
  • ✕Combat log: removed for addons in 12.0

Fair play

  • ✓Shows information only; you press every button
  • ✓Every combat read is secret-safe, so no Lua errors when data is restricted
  • ✓/dnd probe reports exactly what's readable where you are
  • ✕No automation, no unlockers, ever
Addon 2 · Darkndark Talents

The build top players run, for the content you're in

For every spec and every bracket, Darkndark Talents looks at the top 50 players and picks the real, complete build that best matches what most of them take. One click creates a loadout and switches to it.

Data: patch 12.1 · murlok.io + raider.io

Real top-player hero tree shares for Retribution (September 2026). The match percentages are examples.

Seven content types

Mythic+, Raid, Solo Shuffle (also your 1v1 duel build), Arena 2v2 and 3v3, Blitz and Rated BGs. When the top players split between hero talent trees, you get an alternative build for the second tree too.

Switches when you do

Enter a dungeon, raid, arena or battleground and it offers the matching build. It stays quiet if that build is already active or a keystone is running.

Know how far off you are

A match score compares your current talents with each recommended build. You can also save your own talents as the build for any content type.

Gear & stat check

Your secondary stat mix next to the top players' mix, your item level gap, missing enchants and empty gem sockets, all in one list.

Bars and keybinds, laid out for you

After a build change, the build's spells are placed on your visible bars and bound to one consistent template, whichever bar addon you use. Macros and items are never touched, and Undo bars steps back through your last five layouts.

Commands

Everything also lives in Esc → Options → AddOns, and the talent window has a Darkndark button.

Darkndark rotation

/dnd unlockMove and resize panels
/dnd lockLock panels (click-through)
/dnd configOpen settings
/dnd resetReset positions and sizes
/dnd probeShow what combat data is readable right now

Darkndark Talents builds & gear

/dndtOpen or close the window
/dndt gearOpen the Gear & Stats tab
/dndt checkCheck the current content now
/dndt resetReset the window position

Get the addons

Both addons are free and install separately; use one or both. Install them with the CurseForge app, or download the zip and extract it into World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns.
