# Changelog

## v0.2.0 (unreleased)
- Darkndark is listed in Blizzard's addon compartment on the minimap; clicking it opens the settings.
- New defensives bar: personal defensives are now shown separately from major cooldowns.
- New "Hold major cooldowns" option: Blizzard's cooldown suggestions glow on the cooldown bar and the main icon shows the best filler instead.
- Optional sounds when you can interrupt and when the main ability procs.
- Masque support.
- Fixed: the interrupt sound could fail to play again after the icon had been hidden.

## v0.1.0 (unreleased)
- First version for Midnight (12.x).
- Main recommendation built on Blizzard Assisted Combat, with optional spec profile rules layered on top.
- Up to 4 predicted upcoming abilities; procs come first.
- Automatic keybind detection for Blizzard bars, Bartender4, ElvUI, Dominos and macros.
- Cooldown bar that finds each spec's major cooldowns on its own.
- Interrupt helper that shows when the target is casting and glows when your kick is ready.
- Every panel can be moved and resized: drag it, scroll the mouse wheel over it, or pull the corner grip.
- Settings panel in Esc > Options > AddOns > Darkndark.
- `/dnd probe` diagnostics window.
